# Exercises

This Section Contains Practical Exercises Developed Throughout The **OpenCV And Computer Vision** Learning Process.

The Exercises Are Divided Into Two Main Categories: Exercises Provided By The Instructor And Exercises Personally Solved Or Developed During The Learning Process.

---

## 👨‍🏫 Instructor Exercises

This Section Contains Exercises, Examples, And Practical Implementations Provided By The Course Instructor.

They Are Kept As A Reference For Following The Course Content And Understanding The Intended Solutions.

---

## 🧑‍💻 My Exercises

This Section Contains Exercises Personally Solved, Modified, Extended, Or Developed During The Learning Process.

These Exercises May Go Beyond The Original Course Examples And Can Include Additional Experiments Or Alternative Implementations.

---

## 📚 Topics

The Exercises May Cover A Wide Range Of OpenCV And Computer Vision Concepts, Including:

* Image Reading And Display
* Image Properties And Manipulation
* Resizing And Cropping
* Color Spaces
* Color Channels
* HSV
* Alpha Channel And Transparency
* Bitwise Operations
* Masking
* Image Transformations
* Drawing And Annotation
* Filtering And Image Processing
* Object Detection
* Feature Extraction
* And Other Computer Vision Concepts

The Topics Will Expand As The Learning Process Progresses.

## 🧭 Progress

The Exercises Are Added Gradually As New Topics Are Learned And Practiced.
