# Exercises

This Section Contains Practical Exercises And Course Projects Developed Throughout The OpenCV And Computer Vision Learning Journey.

The Content Is Organized Into Three Main Categories To Separate Instructor-Provided Materials, Personally Solved Exercises, And Projects Assigned During The Course.

---

## 👨‍🏫 Instructor Exercises

This Section Contains Exercises, Code Examples, And Practical Implementations Provided By The Course Instructor.

These Materials Serve As References For Following The Course Content And Understanding The Concepts Demonstrated During The Lessons.

---

## 🧑‍💻 My Exercises

This Section Contains Exercises Personally Solved, Modified, Extended, Or Experimentally Developed During The Learning Process.

These Exercises May Include Independent Solutions, Additional Experiments, Alternative Implementations, And Improvements Beyond The Original Course Examples.

---

## 🚀 Course Projects

This Section Contains Projects With Specific Goals And Requirements Introduced Throughout The Course.

Each Project Focuses On Applying Previously Learned Concepts To More Complete And Practical Computer Vision Tasks.

Projects Are Organized Separately From Individual Exercises To Maintain A Clear And Consistent Structure.

---

The Exercises And Course Projects May Cover A Wide Range Of OpenCV And Computer Vision Concepts, Including:

- Image Reading And Display

- Resizing And Cropping
- Color Spaces And Color Channels

- Drawing And Annotation

- Filtering And Image Processing

The Topics May Expand As New Concepts And Projects Are Introduced Throughout The Learning Process.
